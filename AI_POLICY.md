# AI Usage Policy

This repository follows the organization-wide
[Jaeger AI Usage Policy](https://github.com/jaegertracing/jaeger/blob/main/AI_POLICY.md).
The human contributor owns and must understand, test and stand behind all submitted
output. DCO sign-off covers agent-assisted contributions.

Agents may draft code, tests, documentation and review replies. Each reply to
review feedback must be read and individually approved by the human before posting.
Declare AI assistance in the pull request template. The linked policy is the
authoritative source if its requirements change.
